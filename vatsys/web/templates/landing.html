How it works

  1. Upload

    Excel or CSV sales and purchase lists, bank statements, and EcoCash, OneMoney or InnBucks exports. It finds the columns itself, and lists any row it couldn't read, with the reason.

  2. Classify

    Each transaction is sorted as standard-rated, zero-rated, exempt or out of scope, using your own rules and the schedules in the VAT regulations. When it isn't sure, it asks rather than guesses.

  3. Review

    Answer plain questions about the transactions it flagged. Checks before filing catch missing invoice numbers, missing exchange rates and invoices claimed twice.

  4. File

    Download the return and its schedules as Excel and enter the figures in TaRMS. Finalising locks the period, so the figures you filed can't change without a record.

Built for Zimbabwean VAT

US dollars and ZiG kept apart

VAT is worked out separately for each currency, because one can't be set off against the other (s38(4)). ZiG amounts are converted at the rate in force on the day, and the return lists every rate it used.

The rules as they stand

Each transaction is taxed at the rate in force on its date, including the 15.5% rate from the 2026 National Budget. It also handles blocked input tax, mixed-use purchases and imported services.

Every figure can be traced

Click any figure on the return to see the transactions behind it and how each was calculated, down to the original row in your file. Every change is recorded in an audit log.

Deadlines and penalties

The dashboard shows when each return is due, and reminder emails go out 10, 5 and 2 days before. A late return shows an estimate of the penalty building up.

Registration threshold

For a business that isn't registered yet, it tracks taxable sales over the last 12 months against the US$25,000 threshold, and says when registration becomes compulsory (s23).

Your data stays here

Classification runs on this server. Your transactions are not sent to any outside service.

Good to know

  • It prepares the return; you still submit it in ZIMRA TaRMS.
  • The VAT rates, rules and exchange rates it uses can all be changed in the app. Confirm them with your tax adviser before relying on the figures.
  • It works on any phone, tablet or computer, and can be added to your home screen like an app.
